# Cookora homepage concept v2 — Bếp Việt đương đại

Mode: built-in ImageGen. New UI concept informed by a source-linked competitor study; not a copy of a competitor. Main font direction: Plus Jakarta Sans, verified Vietnamese support. Motion is specified separately and demonstrated in the interactive preview; a PNG itself does not animate.

## Generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE exceptional high-fidelity desktop homepage UI screenshot for "cookora.", a Vietnamese cooking and meal-planning app. This is a fresh creative direction after a conservative cream-and-serif food-blog design was rejected as too ordinary. The client explicitly requests impressive SANS-SERIF typography, a distinctive contemporary identity, and motion-ready interactions. Deliver a full desktop webpage, approx 1600 x 2200 pixels, straight-on, no browser frame, no device, no presentation canvas, no annotations. Entire page including footer must fit.

DIRECTION: Contemporary Vietnamese kitchen, bold and playful yet premium. Big confident geometric sans-serif typography like Plus Jakarta Sans ExtraBold for headings, Manrope-like clean sans for supporting UI. Absolutely NO SERIFS ANYWHERE, including the logo. Render all Vietnamese accents carefully. Large display typography with tight but readable tracking, strong alignment, generous whitespace, beautiful asymmetrical image compositions. Product-first UX with a functional ingredient composer at the top. Preserve home-cooking warmth while making the brand feel ownable and energetic.

PALETTE: warm porcelain #FAF8F1, near-black olive #20271E, vivid terracotta-orange #E6512B, fresh herb-lime #DDEF89, subtle warm gray borders. Orange is the main CTA. Lime is a deliberate graphic highlight, not a washed-out beige substitute. Dark olive is used for one section and the footer. Flat, tactile surfaces, restrained 20px corners, almost no shadows. Do not use purple, neon gradients, glassmorphism, busy decorative doodles or lots of identical cards.

FUNCTIONAL CONTEXT: App has ingredient matching, AI recipe suggestions, favorites, weekly meal plans and shopping lists. Its planned 300-topic library is not 300 published recipes; never display this as live stock. Actual recipe examples are drafts. All photography here is illustrative. Make the picture feel motion-ready through modular chips, clearly pressable controls, strong before/after spatial relationships, and a crisp active state; do not add arrows or animation annotations outside the product.

FINAL QUALITY: a memorable award-level cooking product homepage that is feasible to implement in React. Typography must be completely sans-serif, with very strong large headline hierarchy, clean Vietnamese text, realistic control sizes, pixel-perfect margins, expressive contrasting section geometry and vivid natural food photography. Distinct from a generic recipe blog. No copied competitor logos, no fake testimonials, no exaggerated metrics, no unrelated infrastructure, no watermarks.
